What we stand for

Mission, vision and the values in between

Our mission

To make cross-border business accessible to any company with a good product — through cross-border business consultation, international marketing planning, brand advisory, general trading and cross-border e-commerce support delivered with clarity and care.

Our vision

A Hong Kong company that becomes the default operating partner for brands going across borders in Asia and beyond — known for speed, transparency and results that survive the next shipment.

Our values

Clarity over noise — plain answers, written plans, no jargon.
Ownership — we take responsibility for the outcome, not just the task.
Long-term relationships — repeat business over one-off deals.
Care in the details — documentation, timing and follow-through.

Milestones

How we got here

From a Hong Kong trading desk to a cross-border business spanning advisory, marketing, brand, trading and e-commerce.

Foundation

CHOICE Overseas International Co., Limited is established in Hong Kong

The company is set up to provide cross-border business consultation and general trading services from its base in Sha Tin, Hong Kong.

Expansion

International marketing planning and brand advisory join the portfolio

Clients asked for more than logistics, so in-house planning and advisory capabilities were added to support launches rather than only shipments.

Digital

Cross-border e-commerce support goes live

Storefront enablement, catalogue localisation and fulfilment routing are packaged as a standalone service line for online sellers.

Product

The mobile management app ships to the App Store and Google Play

Real-time orders, inventory, supply chain tracking, consulting dashboards and e-commerce management move into one mobile workspace for clients.

Today

One operating partner across the full cross-border lifecycle

Consultation, planning, brand, trading and e-commerce support run under one accountable team, tracked end-to-end in the app.

Compliance & responsibility

Trade has rules. We read them before you do.

Cross-border operations carry obligations in every jurisdiction we touch. We screen counterparties against applicable sanctions and restricted-party lists, prepare documentation that satisfies customs requirements, and work to the advertising, privacy and consumer-protection rules that apply to each market.

Our commitments to privacy, data handling and the use of advertising technologies are set out in full in our legal documents.
